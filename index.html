A new way to remember work

Your brain is for thinking, not for remembering ten site visits. A field officer visits 10 petrol pumps in a day: a damaged signboard at one, a leak at another, an unfinished maintenance job, a safety issue. Snap Notes turns each one into a photo note, so nothing depends on memory.

  • Capture: don't stop your work to write long notes. One photo is enough.
  • Preserve: the photo, header, notes, date and time are kept together, printed on the image.
  • Recall: type any word and find exactly what you saw, and when.

What it does

  • Header with date and time stamped on top of the photo, your notes on the bottom.
  • Searchable history, newest first. Filter by Today, 7 days or 30 days, or by site header.
  • Save & Mail: Gmail opens with recipients, subject, notes and photo filled in.
  • Copy in your gallery under Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes for WhatsApp sharing.
  • Optional Google Drive backup to a folder in your own Drive, Wi-Fi only if you like.
  • Recent headers as one-tap chips, email autocomplete and bullet points.
  • No ads, no tracking, works offline without Drive backup.

Questions

What is Wiz Snap Notes?

A visual memory app for work. You take a photo, type a header and notes, and the app prints them on the photo with the date and time. Every photo note is saved in a searchable history so you can find it again in seconds.

How is it different from a normal notes app or camera?

A notes app keeps text without the picture, and the camera keeps pictures without context. Snap Notes keeps both together: the photo, what it is about, and when you took it, printed on the image and searchable by any word.

Can I use it for petrol pump inspections?

Yes. It was made for site visits at IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, Jio-bp, Nayara and Shell petrol pumps: damaged signboards, leaks, pending maintenance, safety issues and dip or meter readings. It works for any field inspection, maintenance record or site visit.

How do I send a photo note to a dealer?

Add one or more email IDs and tap Save & Mail. Gmail opens with the recipients, subject, notes and stamped photo filled in, and you tap Send. You can also share the photo on WhatsApp or any other app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Without Google Drive backup the app works fully offline and your photos and notes stay on your phone. Drive backup is optional and saves to a folder in your own Drive.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking and no account needed.

Wiz Snap Notes turns every site photo into a clear, searchable report. It is built for petrol pump staff, managers and field teams who need proof of what they saw and a quick way to send it.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap Snap and take the photo.
2. Type a header, for example "HPCL Sector 12, Tank 2 dip". The header is required and is printed across the top of the photo with the date and time.
3. Write your points in Notes. They are printed across the bottom of the photo.
4. Add one or more dealer email IDs if you want, then tap Save & Mail. Gmail opens with the recipients, subject, notes and photo filled in. You tap Send.

SEARCH EVERYTHING YOU WROTE
Every photo note goes into the built-in gallery. Type any word, like "leak", "nozzle 3" or a dealer name, and the matching photos appear instantly with the words highlighted. Filter by Today, 7 days or 30 days, or tap a saved header.

SAVED TO YOUR GALLERY
The stamped photo is also saved to your phone gallery in Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes, so you can share it on WhatsApp or upload it anywhere.

MADE FOR DAILY WORK
- Recent headers as one-tap chips, so you never retype a site name
- Email autocomplete from dealers you mailed before
- "Add point" button for neat bullet notes
- Live preview of the stamp while you type
- Pinch to zoom on any saved photo
- Share to any app, or delete when done

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No sign-in, no ads, no tracking. The app has no internet permission. Your photos and notes stay on your phone. Mail is sent by your own mail app only when you tap Send.

LIGHT AND SMOOTH
The black-and-yellow Wiz design is animated but light on battery and memory, so it runs well on low-cost phones.

Wiz Snap Notes is made by Wiz Labs, makers of Wiz Dip Charts, Wiz Fuel Density and Wiz GPS Camera.

See it. Snap it. Remember it.

Wiz Snap Notes is your visual memory. Instead of trying to remember what you saw, take one photo. The app prints a header with the date and time on top, your notes at the bottom, and keeps it ready to find again in seconds.

Built for anyone who wants to keep a memory: a trip, a shop or mall you visited, a product you liked, where you parked, a place you want to come back to. Also great for work: site visits, inspections, maintenance and audits.

ONE PHOTO, FULL CONTEXT
You walk through a mall and spot a jacket you might buy. You travel and find a cafe you want to revisit. You park on level 3, row D. Or you visit 10 sites at work. With Snap Notes each moment becomes a photo note: what, where and when, printed on the picture itself. Nothing to remember, nothing to type up later.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap Snap and take the photo.
2. Type a header, for example "Block C, Floor 2 inspection". It is printed across the top of the photo with the date and time.
3. Write your points in Notes. They are printed across the bottom of the photo.
4. Tap Save, or add email IDs and tap Save & Mail. Your mail app opens with the recipients, subject, notes and photo filled in. You tap Send.

SEVERAL PHOTOS, ONE VISIT
Tap "+ Snap" to add the next issue under the same header. Each photo has its own note and is stamped "Block C · 1/3". Home shows one card per visit, and "Mail all" sends one email with every photo.

STAMP THE PLACE (OPTIONAL)
Turn on "Stamp location" under the header to print the place name and GPS coordinates on the photo. The email gets a Google Maps link. No GPS signal? The photo is saved without location, nothing breaks.

FIND ANY MEMORY IN SECONDS
Every photo note goes into your own searchable history. Type any word, like "leak", "gate 3" or a client name, and the matching photos appear instantly with the words highlighted. The timeline groups notes by Today, Yesterday and date. Filter by 7 or 30 days, or tap a saved header to see every visit to that place.

USE IT FOR
- Travel memories, shopping lists and anything you don't want to forget
- Petrol pump and fuel station visits
- Stock, meter and delivery records you need to report
- Real estate viewings, insurance surveys and retail shelf checks
- Construction progress, snag lists and safety checks
- Maintenance and repair records, before and after photos
- Site visits, field inspections and audits with time-stamped proof

SAVED TO YOUR GALLERY
The stamped photo is also saved to your phone gallery in Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes, so you can share it on WhatsApp or upload it anywhere.

BACK UP TO GOOGLE DRIVE (OPTIONAL)
Sign in with Google and every photo note is copied to a "Wiz Snap Notes" folder in your own Drive, with the header and notes in the description so Drive search finds them too. Choose Wi-Fi only to save mobile data. The app can only see the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive.

MADE FOR DAILY WORK
- Recent headers as one-tap chips, so you never retype a place name
- Email autocomplete from people you mailed before
- "Add point" button for neat bullet notes
- Live preview of the stamp while you type
- Pinch to zoom on any saved photo
- Dates and times follow your phone's language

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking, no Wiz Labs servers. Without Drive backup the app works fully offline and your photos and notes stay on your phone. Mail is sent by your own mail app only when you tap Send.

LIGHT AND SMOOTH
The black-and-yellow Wiz design is animated but light on battery and memory, so it runs well on low-cost phones.

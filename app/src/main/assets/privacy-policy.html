Privacy Policy

Wiz Snap Notes

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Version 1.1

Wiz Snap Notes lets you take a photo, add a header and notes that are printed on it, keep it in a searchable gallery, send it by mail and, if you choose, back it up to your own Google Drive. This policy explains what the app uses and where your data goes.

1. Information the app uses

  • Camera: used only to show the preview and take your photo notes.
  • Photos, headers, notes and email addresses you type: stored on your phone so you can search and resend them.
  • Your Google account email (only if you turn on Google Drive backup): shown in the app so you know which Drive is used.

2. Where your data is stored

Everything stays on your device. Each stamped photo is saved in the app's private storage and a copy is added to your phone gallery under Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes. Headers, notes and recipients are kept in a local database inside the app.

3. No tracking, no Wiz Labs servers

The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Wiz Labs has no server and never receives your photos, notes, email addresses or account details. The app uses the internet only for the optional Google Drive backup described below. Without it, the app works fully offline.

4. Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you tap Sign in with Google on the Google Drive backup screen. When it is on:

  • Each stamped photo is uploaded from your phone directly to a Wiz Snap Notes folder in your own Google Drive. The header, notes, date and the mail IDs you added are saved in the file's Drive description so Drive search can find them.
  • The app asks only for the drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files and the folder it created. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.
  • Your files in Drive are stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • You can choose Wi-Fi only uploads, and you can tap Disconnect Google account at any time. That revokes the app's access. Photos already in your Drive stay there until you delete them in Drive.

Wiz Snap Notes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to back up your photo notes to your Drive. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone.

5. Sending mail and sharing

When you tap Send Mail or Share, the app hands the photo, header, notes and the recipients you chose to the mail or sharing app you pick (for example Gmail). That app sends the message under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent until you confirm it there.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting a photo note in the app removes it from the app and, where Android allows, from Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes. A copy already backed up to Google Drive stays in your Drive; delete it there if you want it gone. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage and database; copies in your gallery and Drive stay until you delete them. You can also remove the app's Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

The app is a work tool and is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app.

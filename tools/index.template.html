A new way to remember

Your brain is for thinking, not for remembering everything you see. You spot a jacket in a mall, a cafe on a trip, the row where you parked, or ten site visits in a day. Snap Notes turns each one into a photo note, so nothing depends on memory.

  • Capture: don't stop to write long notes. One photo is enough.
  • Preserve: the photo, header, notes, date and time are kept together, printed on the image.
  • Recall: type any word and find exactly what you saw, and when.

What it does

  • Header with date and time stamped on top of the photo, your notes on the bottom.
  • Several photos under one header, one card per visit, one email with every photo.
  • Optional location stamp: place name and GPS printed on the photo, with a Maps link in the mail.
  • Searchable history with a timeline: Today, Yesterday and by date. Filter by 7 or 30 days, or by header.
  • Save & Mail: your mail app opens with recipients, subject, notes and photo filled in.
  • Copy in your gallery under Pictures/Wiz Snap Notes for WhatsApp sharing.
  • Optional Google Drive backup to a folder in your own Drive, Wi-Fi only if you like.
  • Recent headers as one-tap chips, email autocomplete and bullet points.
  • No ads, no tracking, works offline without Drive backup.

Use it for

  • Trips: places, food, hotels, signs and ideas you want to come back to.
  • Shopping and malls: products, prices and shops you liked, shopping lists.
  • Where you parked, what you lent, what you saw, anything you don't want to forget.
  • Site visits, field inspections and audits with time-stamped proof.
  • Maintenance, repair and construction records with before and after photos.
  • Real estate viewings, insurance surveys, retail shelf checks, petrol pump visits.

Questions

What is Wiz Snap Notes?

A visual memory app for work. You take a photo, type a header and notes, and the app prints them on the photo with the date and time. Every photo note is saved in a searchable history so you can find it again in seconds.

How is it different from a normal notes app or camera?

A notes app keeps text without the picture, and the camera keeps pictures without context. Snap Notes keeps both together: the photo, what it is about, and when you took it, printed on the image and searchable by any word.

Is it only for work?

No. Use it for trips, shopping, malls, parking spots and personal memories, and equally for site visits, inspections, maintenance and audits.

How do I send a photo note to someone?

Add one or more email IDs and tap Save & Mail. Your mail app opens with the recipients, subject, notes and stamped photo filled in, and you tap Send. You can also share the photo on WhatsApp or any other app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Without Google Drive backup the app works fully offline and your photos and notes stay on your phone. Drive backup is optional and saves to a folder in your own Drive.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking and no account needed.
